## App taxonomy

Every retailer is indexed across one or more user-facing requirements:

- Easy closure
- Seated wear
- Low sensory
- Easy footwear
- Device access
- Assisted dressing
- Extended sizing
- Kids

Search also covers retailer focus and notable product types, including magnetic shirts, seated denim, open-back tops, side-opening trousers, AFO-friendly shoes, abdominal-access clothing and sensory-friendly layers.

## Maintenance rules

- Keep links at collection level unless a particular product is central to the feature explanation.
- Do not cache price or stock claims in the app.
- Review retailer descriptions before a public demo if the brand changes its collection structure.
- Magnetic products should retain appropriate implanted-device cautions wherever detailed product recommendations are later introduced.
- A retailer’s inclusion indicates functional relevance, not an endorsement or commercial partnership.
